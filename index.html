More work

Additional projects

Selected examples of the wider range of environmental questions and analytical approaches I've worked with.

Falkenberg · Aerosols and turbulence

Ongoing investigation of particle number concentrations and size distributions, turbulence, and cloud microphysics over a heterogeneous continental landscape.

Extreme weather and carbon monoxide poisoning

Spatial and temporal analysis of carbon monoxide-related deaths and hospitalizations following major winter storms in the United States, compared with hurricane-related conditions.

Graph neural network for atmospheric temperature

Developed and evaluated a graph neural network using meteorological data and radiosonde observations. Predictive performance was limited, highlighting challenges with the available inputs.

Renewable energy and Dunkelflaute

Independent threshold-based analysis of wind and solar generation potential, combining historical electricity-generation records with meteorological reanalysis data.

Pollen and wind turbines

Exploratory work on potential interactions between pollen and wind turbines; available data were insufficient to draw conclusions.
